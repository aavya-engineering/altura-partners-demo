Investment Thesis

Government Contracts as Commercial Launchpads

DARPA, In-Q-Tel, NASA SBIR, and DoD contracts don’t just validate technology - they fund it non-dilutively. Many great deep tech companies start with government contracts before VC. We navigate this for founders to help them convert government R&D dollars into commercial products at scale.
